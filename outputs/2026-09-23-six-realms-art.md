# Six realms atlas asset

Created using the built-in imagegen tool, with the user's attached fantasy map as a style/composition reference. No external API key was used.

Saved production asset: `web/client/public/assets/world/realm-atlas.png`.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background image for an interactive fantasy game WORLD ATLAS. Create a richly painted isometric aerial fantasy continent inspired by the supplied reference's beautiful detailed terrain, miniature castles, coastlines, rivers, clouds, mountains and distinct biomes. Landscape 1536x1024. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO LOGO, NO LABELS, NO UI, NO BORDER. Precisely arrange SIX realms, leaving terrain visible for HTML overlay labels: central green grassland and grand blue-roof white medieval castle at (44% width,49% height); western emerald ancient forest, giant luminous tree and woodland citadel at (22%,34%); far north snowy mountains, glacial lake and ice blue castle at (48%,18%); eastern golden desert dunes with sandstone domed city and oasis at (69%,48%); far east southeastern black volcanic mountains lava rivers and obsidian fortress at (86%,65%); northeast purple cursed rocky highland with gothic citadel at (82%,24%). Deep turquoise ocean wraps irregular continent coastline, southwest harbor villages, tiny islands, bridges, waterfalls, roads connect the six realms, dramatic snowy peaks separating biomes. Rich highly detailed hand painted premium fantasy strategy game map, warm sunlight with painterly atmospheric depth, readable natural geography. Keep all six castles geographically separate, each biome visually distinctive. No names or text anywhere because interface labels will be added in code.

Labels, selection, player indication, routes and monster/service information are implemented as accessible HTML/SVG over the image. The image is illustrative; only labeled realms are currently playable destinations. Additional castles/islands in the illustration are scenery.
